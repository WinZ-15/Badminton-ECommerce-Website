About Us

SmashMart is an online badminton equipment store that offers high-quality rackets, shuttlecocks, and accessories for players of all levels.

This website is developed as a bachelor-level project using Java Servlets, JSP, and MySQL following the MVC architecture.

Our goal is to provide a simple and user-friendly platform where users can browse products, add them to cart, and purchase easily.
