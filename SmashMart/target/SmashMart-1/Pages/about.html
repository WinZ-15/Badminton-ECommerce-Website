About Us

SmashMart is an online badminton equipment store dedicated to providing high-quality rackets, shuttlecocks, and accessories for players of all levels — from beginners to professionals.

This application is developed using Java Servlets, JSP, and MySQL, following the MVC (Model-View-Controller) architecture.

Our goal is to create a simple, user-friendly platform where users can easily:

  • Browse a wide range of products
  • Search and filter items based on preferences
  • Add products to cart
  • Place orders seamlessly

SmashMart focuses on delivering a smooth and efficient shopping experience for badminton enthusiasts.
