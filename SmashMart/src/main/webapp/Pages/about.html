About SmashMart

SmashMart is a modern online platform designed for badminton enthusiasts, offering a wide range of high-quality equipment including rackets, shuttlecocks, shoes, and accessories.

The purpose of this website is to provide users with a simple, fast, and user-friendly shopping experience. Whether you are a beginner learning the game or a professional player, SmashMart helps you find the right products with ease.

This application is built using Java Servlets, JSP, and MySQL, following the MVC (Model-View-Controller) architecture to ensure scalability, maintainability, and efficient performance.

With SmashMart, users can:

  • Explore a variety of badminton products
  • Search and filter products
  • Add items to their cart
  • Place orders quickly and securely

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide high-quality badminton products at affordable prices while ensuring a smooth and user-friendly online shopping experience for every customer.
