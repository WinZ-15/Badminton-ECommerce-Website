Business Rules According to ERD
User–Order Relationship

A User can place many Orders.

Each Order belongs to exactly one User.

Order–Order_Item Relationship

An Order can contain many Order_Items.

Each Order_Item belongs to exactly one Order.

Product–Order_Item Relationship

A Product can appear in many Order_Items.

Each Order_Item references exactly one Product.

Product–Category Relationship

Each Product belongs to exactly one Category.

A Category can contain many Products.

User Attributes

Each User has a unique user_ID.

Users are identified by unique email.

Each User has a role attribute (Admin, Seller, Buyer).

Order Attributes

Each Order has a unique order_ID.

Orders track order_date, total_amount, and status.

Order_Item Attributes

Each Order_Item has a unique order_item_ID.

Order_Items specify quantity and price for the Product ordered.

Product Attributes

Each Product has a unique product_ID.

Products include brand, stock, image, price, name, and description.

Category Attributes

Each Category has a unique category_ID.

Categories are identified by category_name.
